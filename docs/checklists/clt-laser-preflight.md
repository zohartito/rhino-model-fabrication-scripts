# CLT Laser Preflight

Use before sending CLT wall files to the laser cutter.

- Confirm stock size is 12 x 12 in.
- Confirm stock thickness is about 3 mm.
- Confirm drawing scale is physical model scale.
- Confirm `5 ft real = 2.5 in model` score spacing.
- Confirm red cut lines.
- Confirm blue score lines.
- Confirm yellow stock/bed perimeter.
- Confirm window holes match the actual model.
- Confirm sheet labels and assembly labels are readable.
- Confirm no tiny unnecessary fragments were created.
- Confirm the geometry fits the actual laser bed and the plywood sheet.
